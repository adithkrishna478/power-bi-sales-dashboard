# Retail Sales Performance & Profitability Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive **Retail Sales Performance & Profitability Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard analyzes sales, profit, profitability, product performance, categories, regions, and monthly sales trends to provide a clear view of overall business performance.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Monitor overall sales and profit performance
* Track monthly sales trends
* Identify the highest-performing product categories
* Compare sales performance across regions
* Identify top-performing products
* Compare profitability across product categories
* Provide business-focused insights for decision-making

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI**
* **DAX**
* Data visualization
* Business intelligence
* Data analysis

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Key Performance Indicators

* **Total Sales:** 438.25K
* **Total Profit:** 79.96K
* **Profit Margin:** 18.24%

### Visualizations

* Monthly Sales Trend
* Sales by Category
* Sales by Region
* Top 10 Products by Sales
* Profit by Category

## 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard provides insights into:

* Overall revenue and profitability
* Monthly fluctuations in sales
* Category-level sales performance
* Regional sales performance
* Top-performing products
* Differences between sales performance and profitability
